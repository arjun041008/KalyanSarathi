# Privacy Policy for KalyanSarathi

**Last Updated:** 5 November 2025

Thank you for using **KalyanSarathi** ("the App", "we", "our", or "us").

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app, what data (if any) we collect, how it is used, and your choices regarding it.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

KalyanSarathi aims to respect your privacy and collect only minimal data necessary to operate effectively.

### Personal Data

The App may request access to certain personal information (such as your name, email address, or preferences) to enable specific features.

However, we **do not collect, store, or transmit personal data on our servers**. Any data you provide remains on your device and is not shared or sold.

### Non-Personal & Usage Data

The App automatically gathers limited non-personal information for analytics and performance monitoring, including:

* Device type, model, and operating system version
* App version and usage statistics
* Crash logs, performance data, and diagnostic information
* Anonymous identifiers (such as installation ID or session ID)

This data helps us understand how users interact with the app and improve performance, reliability, and user experience.

## 2. Permissions We Use

KalyanSarathi may request permissions to access specific functions of your device to ensure the app works correctly.

These may include:

### Internet Access

Used to enable content and feature updates.

### Device Storage Access

Used for caching app data locally.

### Network Information

Used to monitor connectivity and feature availability.

### Microphone Access (`RECORD_AUDIO`)

Used to enable speech-to-text functionality or voice-based input features.

Audio data is processed in real time and is **not recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers**.

We only request permissions necessary for the app's functionality, and you can deny any permission not essential to the core operation.
